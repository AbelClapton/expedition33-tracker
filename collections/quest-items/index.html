  • Intact Mine

    Location: To find an Intact Mine, walk toward the cliff east of the Demineur’s location to find a series of column-like rock formations you can leap across. Jump across the rock formations to reach the eastern clearing pictured above. While leaping, remember that Clair Obscur features a mantle mechanic, so you don’t need to land perfectly on each platform to advance. But if you fall at any point, you can return to the starting point by climbing up the handholds attached to the western rockface. When you reach the eastern platform, turn to face southward, and you should be able to spot a purple-glowing item in the distance. Next, leap onto the elongated rock formation to your right, and walk to the southern end of the surface before jumping south again to land on a small ledge. Lastly, jump onto the cylinder-like rock formation to your left, and hop toward the purple-glowing item to collect an Intact Mine. Use: Give to Demineur (Nevron)
